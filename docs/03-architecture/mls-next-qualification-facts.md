# MLS NEXT 2026–27 qualification — facts

Short answers for agents and readers. One section per question. Every fact carries a status:
**CONFIRMED** (official 2026–27 source) · **INFERRED** (reasoned, or an older season) · **UNKNOWN** (not published) · **MEASURED** (MissingTable prod data, 2026-11-05).
Claim IDs like `[FX4]` point to the evidence table in [sources.md](../research/notes/mls-next-qualification-2026-27/sources.md). Full analysis: [mls-next-qualification-2026-27.md](mls-next-qualification-2026-27.md).

Main official source, cited as **KD**: [MLS NEXT announces key dates and expanded national events for 2026-27](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season) (undated; SoccerWire repost 2026-10-05).

**No 2026–27 Homegrown or Flex rulebook has been published.** The only 2026–27 rulebook found is the Academy Division's. Treat anything not marked CONFIRMED as unsettled.

## Does playing at MLS NEXT Fest qualify a team for MLS NEXT Cup?

**No.** Fest is not a listed MLS NEXT Cup route for either division in 2026–27 — CONFIRMED by omission (KD) `[FE19]`. No qualification is needed to attend.

- Fest is a scouting and recruiting showcase: "the largest youth soccer scouting and recruiting event in North America." 1,600+ teams, all six age groups U13–U19, about 2,400 matches, Dec 2–13, 2026 at Arizona Athletic Grounds and Red Mountain Park, Mesa — CONFIRMED `[FE1–FE3]`.
- Format, games per team and tie rules for 2026 are not published — UNKNOWN `[FE11]`. In 2025, teams played three games in four days, matched by division and standings — INFERRED (2025) `[FE12, FE14]`.
- In past seasons Fest hosted a separate **Generation adidas Cup** qualifier for Elite Academies; that is a different competition from MLS NEXT Cup — INFERRED (2025–26) `[FE16, GA7]`. Whether it returns at Fest 2026 is UNKNOWN `[OQ18]`.
- For the Academy Division only, Fest games may count toward regular-season standings (AD Rules II.hh) — INFERRED `[FE20]`.

## How does an Allstate Homegrown team (U15–U19) qualify for MLS NEXT Cup?

- The Homegrown field grows **from 32 to 48 teams** in each of U15, U16, U17 and U19, with teams qualifying "based on league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition or MLS NEXT Flex" — CONFIRMED (KD) `[C3]`.
- **How the 48 places split across those three routes is not published**, nor any per-conference allocation, rank cutoff, wild card or rule for declined berths — UNKNOWN `[C4, OQ1, OQ10]`.
- Do not work slot counts backwards from the 48 total. The format changes every year: 16 league + 16 Flex (2024–25) → 32 by unstated routes (2025–26) → 48 by unstated routes (2026–27) `[X10]`.

## What does winning a group at MLS NEXT Flex get you?

**A guaranteed MLS NEXT Cup spot.** "The winner of each group will automatically clinch a spot in 2027 MLS NEXT Cup" — CONFIRMED (KD) `[FX4]`.

- Flex is "the final qualifying competition" for the 2027 Cup — CONFIRMED `[FX2]`.
- Teams in each age group "are split into groups of four and are seeded based on their performance in league matches from September 2026 to March 2027" — CONFIRMED `[FX3]`.
- Flex is a Homegrown route, not an Academy Division route — CONFIRMED `[FX5]`.
- Runners-up: no official text gives them any route — UNKNOWN `[FX8, OQ11]`. In 2022–2026 only group winners qualified — INFERRED `[FX24]`.
- Number of groups, entry criteria, and which teams play in 2027 are not published — UNKNOWN `[FX7, OQ5]`. In 2023 and 2025 entry was by points-per-game rank — INFERRED `[FX18]`.

## What happens when a Flex match ends in a tie?

- **2026–27: not published** — UNKNOWN `[OQ7, OQ8]`.
- In 2023 and 2025, a tie after regulation went to penalties: **shootout winner 2 points, loser 1** — official for those seasons, INFERRED for 2027 `[FX20, FX21]`. A regulation win is assumed to be 3 points; no source states it `[FX22]`.
- An unofficial standings site shows the same 3/2/1/0 scheme in 2026–27 regular-season Flex conference tables — SECONDARY `[FC9]`.
- Group tiebreaker order is not published for any season. Head-to-head decided one 2025 group — SECONDARY `[FX23, FX10]`.

## What are Flex conferences, and what does winning one get you?

- "The **14 regular season Flex Conference winners** will compete for the ability to earn a first-round bye at MLS NEXT Cup. The top four teams in the table will secure a bye, while the remaining 10 teams will enter MLS NEXT Cup without a bye" — CONFIRMED (KD) `[FC1]`.
- So winning a Flex conference most likely **is** a Cup berth, earned before Atlanta — INFERRED from "enter MLS NEXT Cup" `[FC3]`. The bye race itself is CONFIRMED.
- MLS never defines "Flex Conference" or how the bye table is built — UNKNOWN `[FC2, OQ3]`.
- Flex conferences play their own regular-season fixtures and keep their own table, separate from the league conference — SECONDARY / MEASURED `[FC11, FC13]`.
- MissingTable stores them as a separate league named `Flex`, with **13 conferences** at each of U15, U16, U17, U19: Empire, Florida, Frontier, Mid-America (East), Mid-America (West), Mid-Atlantic (North), Mid-Atlantic (South), New England, Northwest, Southeast, Southwest (North), Southwest (South), Turnpike — MEASURED `[FC6, FC7]`.
- **13 vs 14 is unresolved**: MLS says 14, MissingTable and an unofficial mirror both see 13 `[X4, OQ2]`.

## How does a U15 Homegrown Northeast team qualify?

Two routes are clearly described:

1. **Finish 1st in its Flex conference** — Empire, New England or Turnpike. That is a separate table from the 20-team Northeast table. Berth INFERRED, bye race CONFIRMED `[FC1, FC3, NE21]`.
2. **Win its group of four at MLS NEXT Flex** in Atlanta — CONFIRMED `[FX4]`.

"League play" is a named route, but there is no published cutoff or Northeast allocation, and nothing ties a Northeast table finish to a Cup place — UNKNOWN `[NE22, OQ4]`. The Pro Player Pathway route does not exist at U15 `[P11]`.

Northeast facts:

- U15 Northeast (League): 20 teams, 190 matches — a single round-robin, 19 games each — MEASURED `[NE1, NE3]`. U16, U17, U19: 19 teams, 18 games each `[NE2]`.
- Each Northeast team also sits in one Flex conference, so it is in **two tables at once** — MEASURED `[NE7]`. The three conferences cover the 20 Northeast teams plus CF Montreal in New England — INFERRED `[NE16, NE15]`.
- Example: IFA U15 plays in Homegrown Northeast and in Flex New England — MEASURED (MissingTable prod, 2026-11-06).
- For a specific team's current table, schedule or form, use live MissingTable data (mt-agent / mt-mcp), not this page.

## What is the Pro Player Pathway?

- A Homegrown track for MLS club academies only; each plays one age group up (U15→U16, U16→U17, U18→U19) — INFERRED (2025–26 rule, carried forward) `[P4]`.
- It is a 2026–27 Homegrown Cup route; its slot count is not published — CONFIRMED / UNKNOWN `[P6, P7]`.
- PPP conferences exist at **U16, U17 and U19 only**: Central, Northeast, Southeast, West (Pro Player Pathway), 7–8 teams each. Northeast (PPP) is a double round-robin, 12 games each — MEASURED `[P8, P9]`.

## How does an Academy Division team qualify?

Per age group: **20 through league play** (Quality of Play for U13/U14), **2 through an MLS NEXT Cup Play-In**, **10 by winning one of ten regional Cup Qualifiers** — CONFIRMED (KD) `[PI1]`. That sums to 32 `[PI2]`.

- The Play-In is new for 2026–27 "across all age groups" — CONFIRMED `[PI3]`. Its dates, venue, format and eligibility are UNKNOWN `[PI4, OQ9]`. One release places it at Fest; others don't mention a venue — INFERRED `[X5]`.
- The ten regional qualifiers feature more than 1,600 teams; each winner earns a berth — CONFIRMED `[PI6]`. 2026–27 dates: Utah Oct 9–12 · Roseville Oct 30–Nov 1 · Southeast Nov 20–23 · Scottsdale Feb 26–Mar 1 (U13–U14) and Mar 26–29 (U15–U19) · Florida Mar 5–8 · Ohio Apr 9–12 · Mesa, New Jersey and Kansas City Apr 23–26 · Hammonton Apr 30–May 3 — CONFIRMED `[PI7–PI16]`.
- Academy standings: Win 3 / Tie 1 / Loss 0; tiebreakers from head-to-head through coin toss; no penalty kicks in the rulebook — CONFIRMED (AD Rules 2026–27) `[S26, S27]`.

## How do U13 and U14 teams qualify?

- Both divisions qualify U13/U14 on **Quality of Play (QoP) standings**, not win-draw-loss tables — CONFIRMED (KD) `[Q1]`.
- QoP is built by MLS and Taka: every significant player action is rated by location, description and quality, adjusted for opponent strength, and summed over the season — CONFIRMED ([QoP page](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/qualityofplay/)) `[Q2]`.
- The formula and weights are not published; results may influence QoP indirectly — UNKNOWN / INFERRED `[Q7, OQ17]`.

## What is the Generation adidas Cup?

- Mar 25–Apr 2, 2027 at IMG Academy: MLS academies against top international academies, with dedicated spots for Elite Academies; U16 field grows from 40 to 48 — CONFIRMED `[GA1–GA3]`.
- It is **not** a route into MLS NEXT Cup — CONFIRMED by omission `[GA5]`. Elite Academy spots came from Fest in 2025–26 — INFERRED `[GA7, GA10]`.

## Season structure

- Six age groups: U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19 — no U18 — CONFIRMED `[S1]`.
- Two tiers: the **Allstate Homegrown Division** (all 30 MLS academies plus selected Elite Academies) and the **Academy Division** — CONFIRMED `[S4, S5]`.
- Regular season began Sept 5, 2026 — CONFIRMED `[S8]`. Scale: more than 53,000 players, 2,743 teams, 310 clubs, over 27,000 matches `[S6]` (a March release said 318 clubs `[X1]`).
- Homegrown league conferences in MissingTable: Florida, Frontier, Mid-America, Mid-Atlantic, Northeast, Northwest, Southeast, Southwest — MEASURED `[S23]`. Academy conferences named "Northeast" and "New England" are different conferences — CONFIRMED `[S21]`.
