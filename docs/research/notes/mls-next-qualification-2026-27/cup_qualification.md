## 1. MLS NEXT Cup 2027: dates, location, total teams, format

### Takeaway
The 2027 Cup runs June 5–13, 2027 in Cincinnati (dates subject to change) and covers six age groups (U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19). The Homegrown U15–U19 field grows to 48 teams per age group, and the Academy Division gets 32 per age group. MLS has published no bracket or group format for 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED.** Season 2026–27, all divisions. "MLS NEXT Cup presented by Allstate: June 5-13, 2027*", at Voice of America Park, Warren County Sports Park and TQL Stadium in Cincinnati, OH. The asterisk reads "Subject to change". — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [B](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- **CONFIRMED.** The Cup "will feature the top MLS NEXT teams from the regular season competing for a championship across six age groups: U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, and U19." — [B](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- **CONFIRMED.** Homegrown Division (U15–U19): "The Allstate Homegrown Division will expand from 32 to 48 teams in each of the U15 through U19 age groups." — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [SoccerWire, Oct 5 2026](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED.** Academy Division, per age group: 20 through league play, 2 through the Play-In and 10 through the Cup Qualifiers, which totals 32. — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **CONFIRMED.** The Cup "will also welcome back a showcase competition" for rising juniors and seniors. This is not described as a qualification route. — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **Prior season, INFERRED for 2027.** The 2026 Cup (Salt Lake City, from May 23 2026) used single elimination for U15–U19. U13 and U14 had 64 teams per age group, split into Championship and Premier brackets of 32. — [MLS, May 19 2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2026-mls-next-cup-presented-by-allstate-kicks-off-may-23-with-top-clubs-battling-for-titles)
- **CONFIRMED.** Flex conference winners can earn "a first-round bye at MLS NEXT Cup". The 2027 Cup therefore has at least one round in which some teams get byes. — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: With 48 Homegrown teams, first-round byes and a separate Academy pool, U15–U19 probably have separate Homegrown and Academy brackets: 48 Homegrown with byes, and 32 Academy. No source says whether the two divisions play in combined or separate brackets.
- Do NOT add 48 and 32 to get a per-age-group total. No source states a combined total.

### Gaps
- Group or bracket format for 2027, the number of byes per bracket, and U13/U14 field sizes and bracket tiers (Championship/Premier) for 2027 are not published.
- The Homegrown U13/U14 Cup field size for 2027 is not stated.

## 2. Homegrown Division U15–U19 (48 per age group): league play vs Flex vs Pro Player Pathway

### Takeaway
MLS confirms three routes into the 48-team Homegrown field: league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition and MLS NEXT Flex. It does **not** publish how many slots each route gets, any per-conference allocation, wild-card or at-large slots, or a rule for teams that decline.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED.** Homegrown U15–U19 teams qualify "based on league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition or MLS NEXT Flex presented by Allstate." — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **CONFIRMED.** MLS NEXT Flex runs April 29 – May 4, 2027* at the Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center in Atlanta, GA. It is "the final qualifying competition for 2027 MLS NEXT Cup presented by Allstate." — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **CONFIRMED.** Flex format: "Teams in each age group are split into groups of four and are seeded based on their performance in league matches from September 2026 to March 2027. The winner of each group will automatically clinch a spot in 2027 MLS NEXT Cup." — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **CONFIRMED.** Flex byes: "the 14 regular season Flex Conference winners will compete for the ability to earn a first-round bye at MLS NEXT Cup. The top four teams in the table will secure a bye, while the remaining 10 teams will enter MLS NEXT Cup without a bye." — [SoccerWire, Oct 5 2026](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/); [B](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- **CONFIRMED (absence).** Neither official 2026–27 page gives the number of Flex groups, the number of Flex participants, a definition of "Flex Conference", or the number of Pro Player Pathway slots. — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [B](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- **Prior season (2025–26, 32 teams), INFERRED for 2027.** "32 teams will qualify for the tournament based on their position in league play, the Pro Player Pathway or via MLS NEXT Flex". No split was given. — [MLS 2025-26 key dates](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2025-26-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know); [MLS, May 19 2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2026-mls-next-cup-presented-by-allstate-kicks-off-may-23-with-top-clubs-battling-for-titles)
- **Prior season (2024–25), INFERRED and probably outdated.** "16 teams qualify through league standings. The other 16 qualify through MLS NEXT Flex". Flex ran May 9–13, 2025 at Maryland SoccerPlex, with group winners clinching a spot. — [MLS, Aug 29 2024](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-dates-for-2024-25-season)
- **Pro Player Pathway (2024–25 definition), INFERRED for 2026–27.** In 2024–25, MLS academies moved from U15/U17 to U16/U18 age designations, all MLS academies compete in U19, and rosters are "made up of U18 players and a limited number of overage players". This was framed as a bridge to MLS NEXT Pro. No Cup slot count was given. — [MLS, Aug 29 2024](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-dates-for-2024-25-season)
- **CONFIRMED.** For 2026–27, the conference announcement (Mar 9, 2026) lists Academy Division conferences only (20, four of them new) and seven new Homegrown clubs. It says nothing about Cup slot allocation by conference or about "Flex conferences". — [MLS, Mar 9 2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-announces-new-clubs-and-conferences-ahead-of-2026-27-season)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: "Flex Conference" appears to be a regular-season grouping whose 14 winners already hold Cup spots. At Flex they compete only for seeding: 4 byes, and 10 teams without a bye. Other Flex participants play groups of four for Cup spots. If that is right, 14 of the 48 slots go to Flex Conference winners. That is my reading of the release text, not a stated rule.
- INFERRED: The Pro Player Pathway competition probably covers MLS-club academies (U16/U18/U19 designations in 2024–25). How that maps onto the U15/U16/U17/U19 Cup age groups is not stated.
- The breakdown of the 48 slots (league / Flex / PPP) cannot be derived from published sources. Do not back it out of the total.

### Gaps
- No official rule gives slots per route, per-conference allocations, wild cards, at-large spots, tie-breakers, or what happens when a qualified team declines or is ineligible. I found no rulebook. Search terms tried: Pro Player Pathway, Flex Conference, Play-In.
- The number of Flex groups per age group, and therefore the number of Flex-earned spots, is not published.

## 3. Academy Division: 32 per age group (20 league / 2 Play-In / 10 Cup Qualifiers)

### Takeaway
Per age group, 20 Academy teams qualify through league play (Quality of Play standings at U13/U14), 2 through an MLS NEXT Cup Play-In and 10 by winning one of ten regional Cup Qualifier events. This changed from 2025–26, which used 16 via league and 16 via qualifiers. MLS has published nothing on the Play-In: no format, dates or eligibility.

## 4. U13–U14: Quality of Play (QoP) and TAKA

### Takeaway
For 2026–27, U13 and U14 teams in **both** divisions qualify on Quality of Play standings, not on W-L-D tables. QoP is a TAKA video-analytics rating of action quality, adjusted for opponent strength and aggregated across the season. Official pages do not publish the exact formula or how many QoP slots each division or conference gets, apart from the Academy's 20 per age group.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED.** For U13 and U14, "teams in each will qualify based on their Quality of Play standings." This applies to both divisions. "Quality of Play rankings evaluate teams and players beyond traditional win-loss-draw standings. Using Taka's analytical model, the rankings assess the quality of offensive and defensive actions during matches". — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [SoccerWire, Oct 5 2026](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED (official QoP page, undated).** "Taka's models use the location, description, and quality of every significant player action". Scores are "adjusted for quality of opposition, before being aggregated across the season for each team". QoP is "developed by the MLS and Taka", and "Standings for U13 & U14 based on Quality of Play are published." — [MLS QoP page](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/qualityofplay/)
- **CONFIRMED (launch, Apr 2025).** QoP began as a "pilot program" for U13/U14 that ranks teams "rather than the traditional win-draw-loss standings", using "Taka's analytical formula that evaluates game play". — [SoccerWire, Apr 2 2025](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-debuts-quality-of-play-rankings-for-u13-u14-age-groups/); [MLS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-unveils-groundbreaking-quality-of-play-rankings-x1244)
- **Secondary, 2025, unverified for 2026–27.** Rankings are updated every Friday. Each match yields offensive, defensive and overall scores. Youth Sports Business Report says the "top two or three teams per division" by QoP would be invited to the Cup. I did not open that page; this comes from search snippets of [Goal](https://www.goal.com/en/lists/taka-mls-quality-of-play-mlsnext/bltd9d356a54bc60b42) and [Youth Sports Business Report](https://youthsportsbusinessreport.com/?p=10985).
- **CONFIRMED.** TAKA is also the scouting video platform used for MLS NEXT Talent ID (Oct 9–12, 2026, 13 MLS markets, 1,300+ prospects). — [B](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- **Prior season, INFERRED for 2027.** For 2026, Homegrown U13–U14 earned invitations "based on Quality of Play rankings through Taka's analytical assessments", with 64 teams per age group in Championship and Premier brackets. — [MLS, May 19 2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2026-mls-next-cup-presented-by-allstate-kicks-off-may-23-with-top-clubs-battling-for-titles)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: W/L/D do not directly determine U13/U14 qualification by league route. Results may still influence QoP indirectly, and no source says results carry zero weight. Academy U13/U14 teams can still qualify on results through the regional qualifiers: the Scottsdale event has a dedicated U13–U14 window, and the Play-In is not limited by age group.

### Gaps
- The exact QoP formula and weights.
- Homegrown U13/U14 slot count for 2027.
- Whether QoP slots are allocated per conference or nationally.
- Whether Homegrown U13/U14 have any Flex or PPP route.

## 5. Generation adidas Cup 2027

### Takeaway
GA Cup (Mar 25 – Apr 2, 2027, IMG Academy) is a separate international tournament: MLS academies against top international academies, plus selected Elite Academies. The U16 field grows from 40 to 48. No source says it is a route into the MLS NEXT Cup.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED.** Dates and venue: Mar 25 – Apr 2, 2027* (subject to change), IMG Academy, Bradenton, FL. MLS academies and "top international club academies" compete. "The U16 field will expand from 40 to 48 teams, with the new format ensuring that every group features an international club", and the field includes "dedicated spots for Elite Academies". There is also a third Girls Division edition (international clubs against Girls Academy teams). — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **Prior season (2026), INFERRED for 2027.** 88 teams in total: 80 boys (40 at U16 and 40 at U15, in groups of four) and 8 girls. There were 18 international clubs. The Elite and Pro academies (Cedar Stars Academy Bergen, Sockers FC Chicago, Connecticut United FC) qualified "via their performances at MLS NEXT Fest in December 2025". — [MLS 2026 GA Cup release](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-generation-adidas-cup-to-feature-top-international-clubs-expanded-girls-division)
- **Prior season (2025).** Elite Academies earned GA Cup places by winning a 16-team single-elimination bracket at MLS NEXT Fest (U16 and U19 brackets). — [MLS, Aug 29 2024](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-dates-for-2024-25-season)
- **CONFIRMED.** For 2025–26, the official schedule tied Fest to GA Cup ("Elite Academies a chance to qualify for the Generation adidas Cup"), not to MLS NEXT Cup. — [MLS 2025-26 key dates](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2025-26-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: The 2027 "dedicated spots for Elite Academies" will probably again come from MLS NEXT Fest (Dec 2–13, 2026). This is not stated for 2027.
- INFERRED: The 2027 U15 field size is not stated. Expect 40 if unchanged, but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- 2027 Elite Academy selection mechanism and number of spots, the U15 field, and the participant list (not yet published as of these searches).

## 6. Inconsistencies and cautions

### Takeaway
The official 2026–27 releases leave the Homegrown slot breakdown undefined and describe Flex twice in two different ways. They also say U13/U14 "qualify based on QoP" while the Academy also gives those age groups Play-In and Qualifier routes. I found no regulations document to settle any of this.

### Cited Findings
- Flex is described both as "groups of four… winner of each group will automatically clinch a spot" and as "14 regular season Flex Conference winners… compete for… a first-round bye… top four… bye, remaining 10… without a bye", in the same release. — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- The statement that U13/U14 in both divisions qualify on QoP sits beside the Academy rule of "20 through league play (QoP for U13/U14), 2 Play-In, 10 Qualifiers" and a U13–U14-specific Scottsdale qualifier window. — [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- Academy route counts changed year to year: 16+16 in 2025–26, and 20+2+10 in 2026–27. The number of qualifier events went from 8 to 10. — [MLS 2025-26 key dates](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2025-26-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know); [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- Homegrown route counts also changed: 16 league + 16 Flex in 2024–25, then 32 by unspecified routes in 2025–26, then 48 by unspecified routes in 2026–27. — [MLS, Aug 29 2024](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-dates-for-2024-25-season); [A](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- The Mar 9, 2026 conference release says "47 new clubs" for the Academy Division but lists 49 names, two of which also appear as Homegrown joiners. — [MLS, Mar 9 2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-announces-new-clubs-and-conferences-ahead-of-2026-27-season)
- Ignore an unrelated "MLS adjusts Cup qualifying criteria" (Yahoo, Jan 2025) result. It concerns the MLS first team's Leagues Cup and U.S. Open Cup, not MLS NEXT. — [Yahoo/WCMH](https://sports.yahoo.com/mls-adjusts-cup-qualifying-criteria-200520230.html)

### Inferences
- A reconciled reading of Flex: Flex hosts two competitions. One is a bye-seeding mini-table among the 14 Flex Conference winners, who are already qualified. The other is groups of four in which each winner earns a spot. INFERRED only.

### Gaps
- No official MLS NEXT 2026–27 Cup regulations, handbook or rules PDF was found. Clubs may receive such rules privately.
- Nothing published on declines, replacements, wild cards or at-large spots for any division.
