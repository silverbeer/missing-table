## 1. Age groups U13–U19 in 2026–27

### Takeaway
MLS NEXT competes in six age groups in 2026–27: U13, U14, U15, U16, U17 and U19. There is no U18 competition age group. MLS academy U18 teams play in U19, and Pro Player Pathway teams play one age group up. No age-group change for 2026–27 was announced.

### Cited Findings
- MLS NEXT Cup 2027 (June 5–13, 2027, Cincinnati, OH, "subject to change") covers six age groups: U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19. CONFIRMED for 2026–27, both divisions. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- MLS NEXT Fest 2026 covers U13 to U19, and the schedule and standings pages list U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19. CONFIRMED. — [KICKOFF](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-kicks-off-2026-27-season-developing-future-stars); the official standings page offers selectors for the same six age groups under both "Allstate Homegrown Division" and "Academy Division" — [mlssoccer.com standings](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/standings/) (fetched 2026-11-05; no season label beyond ©2026)
- Homegrown clubs announced for 2026–27 are listed with age groups U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19. Sporting City is listed with U13 and U14 only. CONFIRMED. — [CLUBS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-new-clubs-and-conferences-ahead-of-2026-27-season), 2026-03-09
- In 2024–25, MLS academies moved from U15/U17 designations to U16/U18. U18 teams play in the U19 age group at Fest, Flex and Cup, so they can roster more overage players. This is the origin of the "no U18 bracket" structure. Status for 2026–27: INFERRED (carried forward). — [PPP-2425](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-key-dates-for-2024-25-season/), 2024-08-29
- From 2025–26, MLS clubs may add a third Pro Player Pathway age group (U15). Each MLS academy team plays one group up: U15 in U16, U16 in U17, U18 in U19. — [INIT-2526](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-transformative-sporting-initiatives-for-the-2025-26-season), 2025-08-19
- Club-level confirmation for 2026–27: the New England Revolution Academy fields U18, U16, U15, U14 and U13 teams. Its U18, U16 and U15 teams "will once again compete in older age groups, in the U-19, U-17, and U-16 divisions, respectively." This is SECONDARY (club site). The page shows a date of 2025-09-04 but describes the 2026–27 season and a Sept. 5 kickoff, so the date metadata is inconsistent. — [Revolution](https://www.revolutionsoccer.net/news/revolution-academy-announces-roster-for-2026-27-mls-next-season)

### Inferences
- The play-up rule (U15→U16, U16→U17, U18→U19) still applies to MLS academies in 2026–27. The Revolution release supports this, but no official 2026–27 league document restates it.

### Gaps
- No official 2026–27 rules or handbook PDF was found or retrieved. Birth-year cutoffs and overage allowances for 2026–27 are unverified.
- No source states whether Academy Division clubs field U19 or every age group. Academy Division age groups are not listed in [CLUBS].

## 2. Allstate Homegrown Division vs Academy Division

### Takeaway
2026–27 is the second season with two tiers. The Allstate Homegrown Division is the top tier: all MLS academies plus selected non-MLS Elite Academies. The Academy Division is the second tier, made up largely of clubs new to MLS NEXT. Clubs can move up from Academy to Homegrown, and five did so for 2026–27.

### Cited Findings
- "The second season featuring two competition tiers": Allstate Homegrown Division and Academy Division. CONFIRMED for 2026–27. — [KICKOFF](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-kicks-off-2026-27-season-developing-future-stars)
- Scale for 2026–27: "more than 53,000 players, 2,743 teams and 310 clubs, including all 30 MLS academies," playing over 27,000 matches. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season). **Conflict:** [CLUBS] (2026-03-09) says 318 clubs and over 53,000 players across 40 states plus DC. The later figure, 310, is probably post-attrition, but this is unverified.
- The tiers were named in 2025–26. "Homegrown Division" is the renamed top tier, "the top level of competition in the program", tied to the MLS Player Pathway. The "Academy Division" name was chosen to "highlight the expanded opportunities for clubs outside of the MLS ecosystem." In 2025–26 the Homegrown Division held 30 MLS academies plus 122 Elite Academies; 273 clubs in total. — [TIERS-2526](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-next-unveils-names-of-new-competition-tiers-ahead-of-2025-26-season), 2025-08-21
- New Homegrown clubs for 2026–27 (seven): Penn Rising XI, The Island FC, Club Ohio Soccer, Coppermine SC, One FC, SGA, Sporting City. Five of them (Club Ohio, Coppermine, One FC, SGA, Sporting City) are "advancing from the MLS NEXT Academy Division following strong performances both on and off the field." The Island FC joins "as an MLS NEXT Pro Academy." CONFIRMED. — [CLUBS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-new-clubs-and-conferences-ahead-of-2026-27-season)
- 47 new clubs join the Academy Division for 2026–27. The page lists 49 names: Penn Rising XI and The Island FC also appear in the Academy list. — [CLUBS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-new-clubs-and-conferences-ahead-of-2026-27-season)
- How the divisions differ in Cup access for 2026–27:
  - Homegrown U15–U19 teams qualify for the Cup "through league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition, or Flex."
  - The Academy Division sends per age group: 20 teams via league play (Quality of Play for U13/U14), 2 via the MLS NEXT Cup Play-In, and 10 Cup Qualifier winners. That is 32 per age group (my sum).
  - CONFIRMED. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- Homegrown U15–U19 Cup field expands from 32 to 48 teams. CONFIRMED change for 2026–27. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [KICKOFF](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-kicks-off-2026-27-season-developing-future-stars)

### Inferences
- The Homegrown club count for 2026–27 is roughly 152 + 7 = ~159. This is not stated anywhere, and departures or demotions are unknown, so the figure is unverified.
- Promotion from Academy to Homegrown is evidently discretionary, based on "on and off the field" performance. No published formula was found. The clubs release does not mention relegation.

### Gaps
- No published criteria for Academy→Homegrown promotion, or for any Homegrown→Academy movement.
- No official Homegrown club count for 2026–27.

## 4. What is the Pro Player Pathway (PPP)?

### Takeaway
The PPP is the MLS-academy-only track inside the Homegrown Division. MLS academies field teams at designated ages (U15 optional, U16, U18), each playing one age group up, against each other and against Elite Academies. The goal is to smooth the jump to MLS NEXT Pro. For Homegrown U15–U19 Cup qualification it is a distinct route ("the Pro Player Pathway competition") alongside league play and Flex.

### Cited Findings
- 2024–25 restructure, "designed to improve the player pathway by optimizing the transition from the academy level to MLS NEXT Pro." MLS academies moved from U15/U17 to U16/U18. All MLS academies compete in U19, with squads built mainly from U18s plus some older players. — [PPP-2425](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-key-dates-for-2024-25-season/), 2024-08-29
- 2025–26: an optional third PPP age group (U15). Teams play one up (U15→U16, U16→U17, U18→U19), aiming to "create more meaningful competition for both MLS clubs and MLS NEXT Elite Academies." The release does not say how PPP teams are grouped into conferences. — [INIT-2526](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-transformative-sporting-initiatives-for-the-2025-26-season), 2025-08-19
- 2025–26 Cup: the 32 Homegrown U15–U19 qualifiers came "based on their position in league play, the Pro Player Pathway or via MLS NEXT Flex." — [CUP-2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2026-mls-next-cup-presented-by-allstate-kicks-off-may-23-with-top-clubs-battling-for-titles), 2026-05-19
- 2026–27: Homegrown Division Cup entry is "through league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition, or Flex." CONFIRMED that PPP remains a qualification route in 2026–27. The number of Cup slots allocated to PPP is not stated. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- Historical 2024–25 split of the 32-team field: 16 by league table standings and 16 via Flex group winners. — [PPP-2425](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-evolves-pro-player-pathway-announces-key-dates-for-2024-25-season/). Superseded: the 2026–27 field is 48 and has three routes.

### Inferences
- "Northeast (Pro Player Pathway)" is most likely the conference grouping MLS academy PPP teams in the Northeast region (e.g., NE Revolution, NYCFC, Red Bulls, Union, possibly Island FC as an "MLS NEXT Pro Academy"). It differs from "Northeast" (Homegrown Elite Academy clubs) in membership (MLS academies only), in age labelling (teams play one age up) and in Cup access (a separate PPP route). INFERRED. No official page defines it, and the member list is unverified.
- The secondary evidence leaves open whether PPP teams also play cross-over games against same-region Homegrown Elite Academy teams. INFERRED: the 2025–26 release's "meaningful competition for both MLS clubs and … Elite Academies" suggests they do.

### Gaps
- No official definition of the "Pro Player Pathway competition" format: number of PPP conferences, standings method, or how many Cup slots it yields in 2026–27.
- Island FC's "MLS NEXT Pro Academy" status: whether it plays in PPP is unverified.

## 5. Competitions: one paragraph each, and how they connect

### Takeaway
2026–27 flow:
- **Regular season:** conference league play, Sept 2026 to spring 2027.
- **MLS NEXT Fest** (Dec 2026): showcase, plus the new Academy Division Cup Play-In.
- **Regional Cup Qualifiers** (Oct 2026 to May 2027): Academy Division.
- **Generation adidas Cup** (Mar–Apr 2027): international invitational.
- **MLS NEXT Flex** (Apr 29–May 4, 2027): the final Homegrown qualifier, plus a new bye race among Flex Conference winners.
- **MLS NEXT Cup** (June 5–13, 2027, Cincinnati).

### Cited Findings
- **Regular season:** The season began Sept. 5, 2026, with over 27,000 matches. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [Inside World Football 2026-10-07](https://insideworldfootball.com/2026/10/07/mls-next-unveils-its-biggest-ever-season-schedule) (SECONDARY). League-table position is a Cup route in both divisions. For U13/U14 in both divisions, "Quality of Play rankings evaluate teams and players beyond traditional win-loss-draw standings" and decide qualification. U13/U14 QoP is TAKA-based per [CUP-2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2026-mls-next-cup-presented-by-allstate-kicks-off-may-23-with-top-clubs-battling-for-titles). CONFIRMED for 2026–27 per [KEYDATES].
- **Flex Conference concept (NEW 2026–27):** "New this year, the 14 regular season Flex Conference winners will compete for the ability to earn a first-round bye at MLS NEXT Cup"; the top four teams in the table get byes. CONFIRMED. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)

## 6. Structural changes for 2026–27 vs 2025–26 (flagged)

### Takeaway
Six official changes, plus an unchanged six-age-group, two-tier frame.

### Cited Findings
- **CHANGE:** Homegrown U15–U19 MLS NEXT Cup field goes from 32 to 48. — [KEYDATES](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [KICKOFF](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-kicks-off-2026-27-season-developing-future-stars)
- **CHANGE (new):** a Flex Conference winners' race, in which 14 regular-season Flex Conference winners compete and the top 4 earn Cup first-round byes. — [KEYDATES]
- **CHANGE (new):** an Academy Division Cup Play-In at MLS NEXT Fest across all age groups, sending 2 per age group to the Cup. — [KICKOFF]; [KEYDATES]
- **CHANGE:** Academy Cup allocation is 20 league, 2 play-in and 10 qualifier winners. In 2025–26 it was 16 league plus 16 qualifier finalists. — [KEYDATES] vs [CUP-2026](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/2026-mls-next-cup-presented-by-allstate-kicks-off-may-23-with-top-clubs-battling-for-titles)
- **CHANGE:** Four new Academy Division conferences (likely Garden State, Great Lakes North, Sunshine North, Northern California Redwood). 47 new Academy clubs and 7 new Homegrown clubs, 5 of them promoted from Academy. — [CLUBS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-new-clubs-and-conferences-ahead-of-2026-27-season)
- **CHANGE:** Generation adidas Cup U16 goes from 40 to 48 teams, with an international club in every group. — [KEYDATES]
- **Unchanged / confirmed:** two tiers (second season), six age groups U13–U19 without U18, PPP still a Homegrown Cup route, Flex still the final qualifier with group winners advancing, U13/U14 still qualifying by Quality of Play. — [KEYDATES]; [KICKOFF]
- **Related but separate:** MLS NEXT Pro (the professional league) moves to a summer-to-spring calendar, with a 2027 sprint season and its first full season in 2027–28. This does not change MLS NEXT youth structure. SECONDARY. — [WAFF 2026-08-27](https://www.waff.com/2026/08/27/mls-next-pro-shifting-summer-to-spring-schedule-2027/)
- Cup venue moves from Salt Lake City (2026) to Cincinnati (2027). Dates move from May 23–31 to June 5–13. — [CUP-2026]; [KEYDATES]

### Inferences
- With 48 Homegrown teams, 4 first-round byes implies a bracket that starts with a play-in or round of 48. The format is unverified.

### Gaps
- No official statement on changes to Homegrown conference alignment for 2026–27, or whether PPP regions changed.
- Club-count discrepancy: 318 clubs ([CLUBS], March) vs 310 ([KEYDATES]). Unresolved.
