# U15 Allstate Homegrown Northeast Conference (MLS NEXT 2026–27) and its pathway to MLS NEXT Cup 2027

Research date: 2026-11-05. Labels: CONFIRMED = stated by an official MLS NEXT / mlssoccer.com page. INFERRED = my reasoning from cited data. THIRD-PARTY = unofficial aggregator (leaguetables-soccer.pages.dev), which says its tables are computed by the site or mirrored from the league and are not official.

Source-quality caveat: the official mlssoccer.com standings pages are rendered by JavaScript and returned only navigation menus when fetched. All team-level data below therefore comes from one unofficial aggregator, and should be checked against the official MLS NEXT standings / modular11 before anyone relies on it.

## 1. Which clubs are in the U15 Homegrown Northeast conference (and any Pro Player Pathway conference)? How many?

### Takeaway
An unofficial aggregator lists **20 teams** in the U15 Homegrown "Northeast" conference for 2026–27. They are almost all non-MLS clubs from NY/NJ/CT/MA/NH. CF Montreal appears in the New England Flex table but not in the 20-team Northeast table, so the true count may be 21. I found no official roster. A U15 Pro Player Pathway conference does not appear to exist.

## 4. Cup qualification: regular-season berths, Flex opportunities, conference-specific allocations

### Takeaway
Officially, the Homegrown U15 Cup field expands to **48 teams**, filled via "league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition or MLS NEXT Flex". I found **no published number of berths per route, and no allocation for the Northeast conference**. How a Northeast team qualifies is therefore **unresolved**, beyond the confirmed facts below.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED: "The Allstate Homegrown Division will expand from 32 to 48 teams in each of the U15 through U19 age groups, with teams qualifying based on league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition or MLS NEXT Flex presented by Allstate." — [mlssoccer.com key dates](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know); also [national events release](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season). Neither page shows a dated byline, only "©2026 MLS".
- CONFIRMED: at the Flex event, each group-of-four winner automatically qualifies. — same sources
- CONFIRMED: "the 14 regular-season Flex Conference winners will compete for the ability to earn a first-round bye at MLS NEXT Cup. The top four teams in the table will secure a bye, while the remaining 10 teams will enter MLS NEXT Cup without a bye." — [mlssoccer.com key dates](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- Conflict: a SoccerWire summary describes "four first-round byes" for Flex Conference winners. That wording is consistent with the official text. An earlier summary was ambiguous about whether all 14 compete for byes. — [soccerwire national events](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- CONFIRMED, for contrast: in the **Academy Division**, 20 teams per age group qualify through league play, 2 through the Play-In and 10 through Cup Qualifiers. The NJ/Hammonton regionals are Academy-only. — [mlssoccer.com key dates](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: the official wording implies that **winning one's Flex conference (Empire, New England or Turnpike) by the regular-season table qualifies a team directly for MLS NEXT Cup**, because "the remaining 10 teams will enter MLS NEXT Cup without a bye". So a Northeast U15 team's most clearly documented direct route is to **finish 1st in its Flex conference**. That gives 3 Northeast slots via this route if Empire, New England and Turnpike each count as one of the 14. This is an inference, not a published allocation.
- INFERRED: the second documented route is to **win a group of four at the Flex event** (Atlanta, Apr 29–May 4, 2027). The number of groups, how many Northeast teams are invited, and whether conference winners skip the event are not published.
- 48 total − 14 Flex-conference winners leaves 34 slots for Flex group winners, Pro Player Pathway entrants and other "league play" qualifiers. The split is **not published. Do not assume.**
- Northeast conference-table standing (the 20-team table) is **not shown to map to any Cup berth**. Do not assume that a top-N finish in Northeast qualifies.

### Gaps
- Number of berths per route (league play, Pro Player Pathway, Flex event). Unpublished in sources found.
- Whether there is any Northeast-specific allocation. None found.
- Whether there is a U15 Pro Player Pathway at all, and how many U15 berths it takes.
- The official 2026–27 Homegrown rules/regulations document was not located.
