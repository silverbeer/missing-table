# MLS NEXT Flex — 2026–27 season (Flex event April 29–May 4, 2027)

Research date: 2026-11-05. Labels: **CONFIRMED-2026-27** = stated in the official 2026–27 announcement. **CONFIRMED-earlier** = stated officially for an earlier season only, so **INFERRED/unverified for 2026–27**. **SECONDARY** = club, press or aggregator source, not MLS NEXT.

Primary 2026–27 source: MLS NEXT, "MLS NEXT announces key dates and expanded national events for 2026-27 season". The page shows no date; SoccerWire and FootyAccess carried it on 2026-10-05. https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season — mirrored at https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/ (dated Oct 05, 2026).

**I found no official 2026–27 Flex competition rules or regulations PDF.** No official source for any season gives the full group tiebreaker order. Everything below on points and tiebreakers comes from earlier seasons or from secondary sources.

## 1. What is Flex, and which divisions and age groups take part?

### Takeaway
MLS NEXT Flex is a single national event in groups of four. For 2026–27 it is "the final qualifying competition for 2027 MLS NEXT Cup", held April 29–May 4, 2027, in Atlanta. The official announcement ties Flex qualification to the **Allstate Homegrown Division, U15–U19** only. For 2026–27 a separate idea also appears: a regular-season **"Flex Conference"** whose 14 winners play for MLS NEXT Cup byes. The announcement never defines it.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27:** Flex runs April 29–May 4, 2027 (marked "subject to change") at the Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center, Atlanta, GA. It "is the final qualifying competition for 2027 MLS NEXT Cup presented by Allstate". — [MLS NEXT 2026-27 announcement](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [SoccerWire, 2026-10-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27:** The Allstate Homegrown Division expands "from 32 to 48 teams in each of the U15 through U19 age groups". Teams qualify "based on league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition or MLS NEXT Flex". — same sources
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27:** Academy Division teams qualify for MLS NEXT Cup by other routes, and Flex is not listed among them. Per age group, 20 qualify via league play (Quality of Play for U13/U14), 2 via the MLS NEXT Cup Play-In and 10 by winning an MLS NEXT Cup Qualifier event. — [SoccerWire, 2026-10-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27:** The 2027 MLS NEXT Cup runs June 5–13, 2027, in Cincinnati (Voice of America Park, Warren County Sports Park, TQL Stadium) for U13, U14, U15, U16, U17 and U19. — [SoccerWire, 2026-10-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2026 Flex, 2025–26 season):** Flex ran April 23–28, 2026, at the Toyota Soccer Center/Complex in Frisco, TX, as the final qualifying competition for the 2026 MLS NEXT Cup in Salt Lake City (May 23–31). — [St. Louis CITY SC (SECONDARY, club)](https://www.stlcitysc.com/news/st-louis-city-sc-academy-teams-to-participate-in-2026-mls-next-flex-in-frisco-texas); [Inter Miami CF (SECONDARY, club)](https://www.intermiamicf.com/news/inter-miami-cf-academy-takes-on-the-2026-mls-next-flex-in-frisco-tx)
- **SECONDARY, 2026:** Inter Miami listed its 2026 Flex teams by division as "MLS NEXT Allstate Homegrown U-19 / U-17 / U-16 Division". This suggests the 2026 Flex was already a Homegrown-division event. — [Inter Miami CF](https://www.intermiamicf.com/news/inter-miami-cf-academy-takes-on-the-2026-mls-next-flex-in-frisco-tx)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2025):** Flex 2025 (May 9–13, Maryland SoccerPlex) had 232 teams across U15, U16, U17 and U19. — [SoccerWire, 2025-05-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/)

### Inferences
- Flex for 2026–27 is most likely a Homegrown Division event for U15, U16, U17 and U19. The 2027 Cup lists no U18, while the Homegrown expansion text says "U15 through U19". Flex is not an Academy Division route. INFERRED: the announcement does not state the Flex age groups explicitly.
- The "Flex Conference" looks like a regular-season conference grouping of Homegrown teams, possibly the pool that feeds the Flex event. It is not defined anywhere I could find. Do not treat it as a synonym for the Flex event.

### Gaps
- The definition and membership of the "Flex Conference", and why there are exactly 14. No official text explains it.
- The exact Flex age-group list for 2027. U13/U14 are presumably excluded but this is not stated.
- Whether there is any **fall 2026 Flex play**. I found no fall 2026 Flex event in the 2026–27 schedule announcement. The only Flex dates listed are April 29–May 4, 2027.

## 2. Who qualifies for Flex, how teams are seeded, and how conferences are represented

### Takeaway
For 2026–27, teams in each age group are split into groups of four, seeded on league performance from September 2026 to March 2027 (CONFIRMED). The official text does not give the entry criteria (how many teams, from which conferences), the seeding method or any conference quotas.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27:** "Teams in each age group are split into groups of four and are seeded based on their performance in league matches from September 2026 to March 2027." — [SoccerWire, 2026-10-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2026):** The same wording appeared for September 2025 to March 2026. — [Philadelphia Union (SECONDARY, club)](https://philadelphiaunion.com/academy/news/union-academy-heads-to-mls-next-flex-looking-to-secure-postseason-berths)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2025, 2023):** Participants qualified by "points-per-game rank" in the regular-season division standings. — [SoccerWire, 2025-05-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/); [MLS 2023 Flex article](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-clubs-look-to-clinch-postseason-berth-at-mls-next-flex)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2025):** One article says the 2025 teams "were drawn into groups". It does not describe the seeding method. — [SoccerWire, 2025-05-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2025, 2026):** Each team plays three matches "against clubs outside its regular season division". — [SoccerWire, 2025-05-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/)

### Inferences
- Using PPG as the seeding metric for 2026–27 is INFERRED from 2023 and 2025. The 2026–27 text says only "performance in league matches".
- Avoiding same-division opponents in a group is INFERRED from 2025.

### Gaps
- The number of Flex entrants per age group for 2027, any conference or division quotas, the pot structure, and whether teams already qualified via league play are excluded. None of this is in official 2026–27 text.

## 4. Group standings: points and tiebreakers

### Takeaway
No official source for any season states the full points table or the tiebreaker order. Head-to-head is attested as a group tiebreaker (SECONDARY, 2025). Under the 2023 and 2025 rule, a shootout winner earns 2 points and the loser 1. A regulation win is presumably 3 points, but no source I found states this.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2025):** For games tied after regulation, "the winner of penalty kicks will earn two points, and the loser will receive one point." — [SoccerWire, 2025-05-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2023):** "The winner of penalty kicks will earn two points, while the loser will take home one point." — [MLS 2023 Flex article](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-clubs-look-to-clinch-postseason-berth-at-mls-next-flex)
- **SECONDARY (2025):** In one group, LAFC finished first over Minnesota United on a head-to-head tiebreaker after beating them 2–1. — [Minnesota United, "Loons fall just short at MLS NEXT Flex"](https://www.mnufc.com/news/loons-fall-just-short-at-mls-next-flex)

### Inferences
- Win 3 / shootout win 2 / shootout loss 1 / loss 0 is the most likely scheme. The 3 for a regulation win is INFERRED, not found in text. The whole scheme is INFERRED/unverified for 2026–27.
- Head-to-head as an early tiebreaker is INFERRED from a single secondary report. Its position in the order, and whether goal difference or goals scored follow it, is unknown.

### Gaps
- The official tiebreaker order (head-to-head, goal difference, goals for, goals against, fair play, draw) for any Flex season. I looked for it on mlssoccer.com and in club previews and did not find it. The standings pages at mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/tournaments/flex/standings render no data without JavaScript.
- Whether shootout goals count toward goal difference. Presumably not, but this is unverified.

## 5. Are tied group matches decided by penalty kicks? Do shootouts carry bonus points?

### Takeaway
Yes, in 2023 and 2025 (official), and shootouts were observed in 2026. A tied match goes straight to penalties: winner 2 points, loser 1. For 2026–27 this is **INFERRED/unverified**. The 2026–27 announcement does not mention it.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2023, 2025):** See section 4. Ties after regulation are decided by penalty kicks, 2 points to the winner and 1 to the loser. — [MLS 2023](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-clubs-look-to-clinch-postseason-berth-at-mls-next-flex); [SoccerWire 2025](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/)
- **SECONDARY, observed 2026:** The St. Louis CITY U17s "drew 3-3 with New York SC in regulation, then lost 5-4 on penalty kicks" at the 2026 Flex. — [St. Louis CITY SC recap](https://www.stlcitysc.com/news/academy-recap-2026-mls-next-flex-scores-and-results-u-19s-qualify-for-mls-next-cup-in-may)

### Inferences
- The shootout rule probably continued through 2026 and will probably hold for 2027. INFERRED.

### Gaps
- No 2026–27 regulation text confirms shootouts or the 2-and-1 point split.

## 6. Do group winners qualify for MLS NEXT Cup automatically, and are there other pathways?

### Takeaway
Yes. "The winner of each group will automatically clinch a spot in 2027 MLS NEXT Cup" (CONFIRMED-2026-27). No official text mentions runners-up, wild cards or at-large spots from Flex. Separately, and new for 2026–27, the 14 regular-season Flex Conference winners compete for first-round byes at the Cup.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27:** "The winner of each group will automatically clinch a spot in 2027 MLS NEXT Cup presented by Allstate." — [MLS NEXT announcement](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **CONFIRMED-2026-27 ("new this year"):** "The 14 regular season Flex Conference winners will compete for the ability to earn a first-round bye at MLS NEXT Cup. The top four teams in the table will secure a bye, while the remaining 10 teams will enter MLS NEXT Cup without a bye." — [SoccerWire, 2026-10-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/); [MLS NEXT announcement](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier:** Only group winners qualified in 2022, 2023, 2025 and 2026. — [MLS 2023 qualification](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2023-mls-next-cup-playoffs-qualification); [SoccerWire 2025](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/); [SoccerJournal 2026-04-09 (SECONDARY)](https://www.soccerjournal.com/mls-next-flex-takeaways-from-the-u-15-and-u-16-group-draws/)
- **SECONDARY, 2026:** St. Louis U17s went 2W plus one shootout loss and finished second. They did not qualify. — [St. Louis CITY SC recap](https://www.stlcitysc.com/news/academy-recap-2026-mls-next-flex-scores-and-results-u-19s-qualify-for-mls-next-cup-in-may)

### Inferences
- Reading the text literally, the 14 Flex Conference winners are already qualified for the Cup through their regular-season conference title, so the Flex event decides only their seeding for byes. Whether they are in the bye-competition "table" instead of, or alongside, the qualifying groups is not explained. **INFERRED; unclear.** "Top four teams in the table" suggests a single ranking table across the 14 conference winners at Flex, perhaps by Flex points. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- How the 14-team bye table is built (one combined table, or across groups?), and its tiebreakers.
- Whether the bye competition runs in every age group or only some.
- Whether second-place teams have any route to the Cup. None is documented.

## 7. Worked example: a four-team group

### Takeaway
The illustration below uses the only documented scoring (2023 and 2025). It is **INFERRED for 2027**, with 3 points per regulation win ASSUMED. The group winner (first place) clinches a Cup spot.

### Cited Findings
- Scoring basis: a tie goes to penalties, 2 points to the winner and 1 to the loser. — [SoccerWire 2025](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/). Group winner qualifies. — [MLS NEXT 2026-27](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season). Head-to-head is used as a tiebreaker. — [MNUFC (SECONDARY)](https://www.mnufc.com/news/loons-fall-just-short-at-mls-next-flex)

### Inferences
Hypothetical Group X, teams A, B, C, D, with six matches:

| Match | Result | Points |
|---|---|---|
| A v B | 2–1 A | A 3, B 0 |
| C v D | 1–1, C wins on PKs | C 2, D 1 |
| A v C | 0–0, C wins on PKs | C 2, A 1 |
| B v D | 3–0 B | B 3, D 0 |
| A v D | 2–0 A | A 3, D 0 |
| B v C | 2–1 B | B 3, C 0 |

Final points: A 7, B 6, C 4, D 1. **A wins the group and clinches the 2027 MLS NEXT Cup spot.** B gets nothing, because no runner-up pathway is documented.

Variant with a tie on points: if B had beaten A instead, A would have 4 and B 9. In a scenario where A and B finish level on points, the head-to-head winner would rank first, if head-to-head is the first tiebreaker as the 2025 MNUFC report implies. If head-to-head is unresolved (for example in a three-way tie), the next criteria (GD, goals for) are **unknown**.

Two points to note: the shootout scheme means a group never has a 1-point "draw" for both sides, and total points per match are 3 either way.

### Gaps
- The full tiebreaker chain is needed to resolve three-way ties. It is undocumented.

## 8. Do Flex results count in regular-season conference standings?

### Takeaway
Probably not. Flex is described as a separate national event in which teams face clubs outside their regular-season division, and seeding uses league matches through March. No source states this outright.

### Cited Findings
- Seeding is "based on their performance in league matches from September 2026 to March 2027". League play is the input to Flex, and the source never describes Flex as part of league play. — [SoccerWire, 2026-10-05](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- **CONFIRMED-earlier (2025):** Teams play three matches against clubs outside their regular-season division. — [SoccerWire 2025](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-flex-2025-showcase-ready-to-feature-232-elite-teams-in-maryland/)

### Inferences
- That Flex results stay out of conference standings is INFERRED. Separately, the regular season ends in March, before Flex (April 29–May 4).

### Gaps
- No explicit official statement. Also unclear: whether "Flex Conference" standings, the regular-season table, include matches played at the Flex event. This matters for how the 14 conference winners are determined.
