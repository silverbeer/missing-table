## 1. Dates, location, age groups and divisions of MLS NEXT Fest 2026

### Takeaway
MLS NEXT Fest 2026 runs **December 2–13, 2026** at **Arizona Athletic Grounds and Red Mountain Park in Mesa, Arizona**. It covers all six age groups (U13–U19), with more than 1,600 teams from at least 300 clubs. Official sources do not say which divisions take part. The event appears on the official **Academy Division** events calendar, and a Homegrown club (MNUFC) also lists it, so both divisions very likely take part (INFERRED for 2026; CONFIRMED for 2025).

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27, all ages: "December 2-13, 2026", "Arizona Athletic Grounds and Red Mountain Park - Mesa, Arizona". Described as "the largest youth soccer scouting and recruiting event in North America." — [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know); [OFF-1](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27: 12 days; more than 1,600 teams from at least 300 clubs; all six age groups U13–U19; more than 32,000 players; about 2,400 matches; hundreds of professional, national-team and college scouts. — [OFF-1](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [SEC-1, Oct 5 2026](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27, Academy Division: the official Academy Division events calendar lists "MLS NEXT Fest: December 2-13, Arizona Athletic Grounds and Red Mountain Park, Mesa, AZ", with no further description. — [OFF-3](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/academy-division/events)
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27, Academy Division: the official Academy Division page calls Fest one of "Two marquee national events: MLS NEXT Fest and MLS NEXT Cup." — [Academy Division page](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/academy-division/)
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27, Academy Division: the AD Rules & Regulations define "MLS NEXT Academy Division Competition" as "friendly matches, Regular Season matches, MLS NEXT Fest, MLS NEXT Academy Division Regional Events, and MLS NEXT Cup (each as applicable)" (Sec. II.s). — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf)
- Homegrown Division (Minnesota United, club site): the 2026–27 schedule lists MLS NEXT Fest, Dec 2–13, Mesa, among the national tournaments the squads' league and Flex play gives "paths to qualifying for". — [CLUB-2](https://www.mnufc.com/news/minnesota-united-academy-announces-2026-2027-mls-next-schedule)
- 2025 comparison (CONFIRMED for 2025 only): Dec 4–15, 2025, Mesa. U15–U19 played at Arizona Athletic Grounds; U13–U14 played at Scottsdale Sports Complex, Bell 94 and Reata. 1,474 teams from 250+ clubs, Homegrown and Academy divisions together. — [SEC-2, Nov 25 2025](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-fest-2025-to-become-largest-talent-id-event-in-u-s-with-30000-players/); [CLUB-1](https://www.philadelphiaunion.com/academy/news/philadelphia-union-academy-to-compete-at-2025-mls-next-fest)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: both the Homegrown and Academy divisions take part in 2026. The evidence is the AD calendar and AD rules listing, a Homegrown club's schedule, the 2025 precedent, and a team count (1,600+) that only fits with both divisions.
- INFERRED: the U13/U14 games may again be split off to Scottsdale-area venues, as in 2025. The 2026 announcement names only Mesa venues (Arizona Athletic Grounds and Red Mountain Park), so this is unverified.

### Gaps

## 2. Selection and invitation: automatic or qualification-based?

### Takeaway
No 2026 source explains how teams are selected. The 2025 evidence and the size of the event (1,600+ teams against roughly 2,000+ program teams) point to near-blanket participation by member clubs rather than earned qualification. This is INFERRED and not confirmed for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 2026: the official announcement and the SoccerWire repost do not describe selection or format. — [OFF-1](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [SEC-1](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-announces-details-on-expanded-national-events-slate-for-2026-27/)
- 2025 only: "matches are scheduled based on division, past event competition, and MLS NEXT regular-season standings." The article does not describe any qualification needed to attend the main event. — [SEC-2](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-fest-2025-to-become-largest-talent-id-event-in-u-s-with-30000-players/); [CLUB-1](https://www.philadelphiaunion.com/academy/news/philadelphia-union-academy-to-compete-at-2025-mls-next-fest)
- AD Rules 2026–27, Sec. II.v: "Club participation is required at MLS NEXT Academy Division Regional Events, which are part of the Regular Season." Fest is defined separately from "Regional Events" in Sec. II.s. — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Fest attendance is invitational or program-wide and not earned through results. Matchups are seeded by standings, at least in 2025.
- Important nuance, INFERRED: AD Rules Sec. II.hh defines the Regular Season as "inclusive of regional and winter MLS NEXT Academy Division Events, and counting towards points for regular season standings." Fest is the December (winter) national event, so **Academy Division Fest games may count toward regular-season league standings**. If so, Fest would feed MLS NEXT Cup qualification **indirectly**, through the 20 league-play spots. No source explicitly says that Fest results count. This needs confirmation.

### Gaps
- Whether participation is mandatory, optional or invitation-only for each division in 2026.
- Whether Academy Division Fest results count in conference standings. Homegrown is the same question and is not covered by these documents.

## 3. Games per team

### Takeaway
In 2025, main-event teams played **three games in four days**. No 2026 source states a games-per-team figure or a guaranteed minimum.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 only: "three games in four days" for main-event teams. — [SEC-2](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-fest-2025-to-become-largest-talent-id-event-in-u-s-with-30000-players/); [CLUB-1](https://www.philadelphiaunion.com/academy/news/philadelphia-union-academy-to-compete-at-2025-mls-next-fest) (Union U-15, U-16 and U-18 each listed 3 matches)
- 2026: about 2,400 matches for more than 1,600 teams. — [OFF-1](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season)
- AD Rules Sec. XIII.c.ii: "Clubs may not request to schedule three (3) Matches in four (4) days during Regular Season competition." This limits club requests and does not bind league-run events. — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: 2,400 matches × 2 teams ÷ 1,600 teams ≈ 3 games per team, which matches the 2025 "three games" model. Unverified for 2026.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 guaranteed-minimum statement.

## 4. Showcase, standings or qualification? Elimination rounds?

### Takeaway
The main Fest event is described officially as a scouting and recruiting showcase. No 2026 source describes group standings or knockouts for the main event. In 2025, Fest **also hosted a separate single-elimination qualification competition**: the Generation adidas Cup Qualifiers for U15/U16, which are separate from MLS NEXT Cup. No 2026 source says whether that sub-competition returns.

### Cited Findings
- 2026: officially framed as "the largest youth soccer scouting and recruiting event in North America", with no format details. — [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- 2025 only (CONFIRMED for 2025): "The 2026 Generation adidas Cup Qualifiers return for U15 and U16, expanding from 16 to 32 teams per age group." Teams "qualify by finishing the fall in qualifying positions in their division's power ranking". The format was single elimination, and each bracket winner earned a spot in the 2026 Generation adidas Cup. Three U15 MLS NEXT Pro academy teams also played, with the top one qualifying. — [SEC-2](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-fest-2025-to-become-largest-talent-id-event-in-u-s-with-30000-players/); [CLUB-1](https://www.philadelphiaunion.com/academy/news/philadelphia-union-academy-to-compete-at-2025-mls-next-fest)
- 2025 only: side events included two U17 "Best Of" games and four U19 "Uncommitted Matches". — [SEC-2](https://www.soccerwire.com/news/mls-next-fest-2025-to-become-largest-talent-id-event-in-u-s-with-30000-players/)
- 2025 Fest secondary summary (search-level, not fetched in full): teams were placed in groups of four and played round-robin. This conflicts with SEC-2, which describes "scheduled matches" without groups. Treat as unverified.
- The 2027 Generation adidas Cup runs March 25–April 2, 2027 in Bradenton, FL. — [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: the main event is showcase-style, with seeded matchups and no titles or knockouts. A 2026 GA Cup qualifier at Fest is plausible given the 2025 precedent but is **unverified**.

### Gaps
- No 2026 confirmation of any qualifier held at Fest, whether for the GA Cup or MLS NEXT Cup.

## 5. Academy Division MLS NEXT Cup Play-In (2026–27)

### Takeaway
CONFIRMED: the 2026–27 season adds an **MLS NEXT Cup Play-In** in the Academy Division that awards **2 MLS NEXT Cup spots per age group**. The other routes are 20 spots from league play and 10 from Qualifier wins, for 32 per age group. No official source gives its dates, location, format, eligibility or age-group coverage. **No source links the Play-In to MLS NEXT Fest.** It does not appear on the AD events calendar, and the AD Rules & Regulations PDF does not mention it.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27, Academy Division, per age group: "20 teams will qualify for MLS NEXT Cup Presented by Allstate through league play (QoP for U13/U14), two teams will qualify through the MLS NEXT Cup Play-In, and 10 teams will qualify by winning one of the MLS NEXT Cup Qualifier events." — [OFF-1](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27: ten Academy Division Cup Qualifier regionals: Utah (Oct 9–12), Roseville (Oct 30–Nov 1), Southeast (Nov 20–23), Florida (Mar 5–8), Scottsdale (U13–U14 Feb 26–Mar 1; U15–U19 Mar 26–29), Ohio (Apr 9–12), Mesa (Apr 23–26), New Jersey (Apr 23–26), Kansas (Apr 23–26), Hammonton (Apr 30–May 3). None is labelled a Play-In, and none falls in the Fest window. — [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know); [OFF-3](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/academy-division/events)
- CONFIRMED: the AD events calendar lists the 10 regionals, Fest and the Cup (June 5–13, Cincinnati). **No Play-In is listed.** — [OFF-3](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/academy-division/events)
- CONFIRMED: the AD Rules & Regulations 2026-2027 contain no "Play-In" text. The terms searched were "play-in", "penalt", "extra time" and "tied". The document defines Post-Season as "The MLS NEXT Cup" (Sec. II.cc). — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf)
- 2025–26 contrast (secondary search summary): the prior AD format was 16 Cup spots from league play plus 16 Qualifier finalists, with no Play-In. The Play-In is new for 2026–27. — summary drawn from [Academy Division page](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/academy-division/) and [Inside World Football, Oct 7 2026](https://insideworldfootball.com/2026/10/07/mls-next-unveils-its-biggest-ever-season-schedule). Not independently verified.
- Internal inconsistency in official docs: AD Rules Sec. II.y defines MLS NEXT Cup as among "qualifying Teams from the U15 through U19 age groups". The AD Technical Standards list "Playoff Age Groups ... U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, and U19", and OFF-1 includes U13/U14 qualification via QoP. — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf); [OFF-5](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786479556/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Technical_Standards_2026-27_Final_ipzcjw.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: "two teams through the Play-In" is stated "in each age group", which implies the Play-In covers all six Academy age groups (U13–U19). Not explicitly stated.
- INFERRED: the Play-In is likely a post-regular-season event for teams just outside the 20 league-play spots, as with play-ins generally. **Unverified; no source describes it.** Do not assume it is held at Fest. Fest falls mid-regular-season (December), before league standings are final, which makes a Fest-hosted Play-In unlikely.

## 6. Tied-game rules (showcase and play-in)

### Takeaway
No source found states the tie rules for Fest showcase games or the Play-In. The AD Rules give regular-season points for ties (Tie = 1), so drawn league games stand as draws.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27, AD: standings points are "Win 3 / Tie 1 / Loss 0 / Forfeit 0–3 loss" (Sec. XIII.k). Regular-season tiebreakers run from head-to-head through coin toss (Sec. XIII.l). No penalty-kick provision appears in the Rules (searched). — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf)
- AD Technical Standards 2026–27: no penalty, extra-time or tie terms appear (searched). — [OFF-5](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786479556/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Technical_Standards_2026-27_Final_ipzcjw.pdf)
- Historical, different competition: MLS NEXT Cup Playoffs 2021 guidelines sent ties to penalty kicks, except championship matches, which got extra time. — [MLS NEXT Cup Playoffs 2021 guidelines](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-cup-playoffs-2021-competition-guidelines). Not applicable to Fest or the 2026 Play-In.

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Fest showcase games most likely end as draws with no shootout, consistent with a showcase and with the AD standings counting ties. **Unverified.** Single-elimination sub-events (such as the 2025 GA Cup qualifiers) must produce a winner, but their method (PKs or extra time) is not stated in any source found.

### Gaps
- Fest 2026 match rules or event guidelines document: not found.
- Play-In tie-breaking: not found.

## 7. Does Fest participation itself confer MLS NEXT Cup qualification?

### Takeaway
**No evidence that it does.** The official qualification paths for 2026–27 are listed in full, and Fest is not one of them:
- Academy: league play (20), Play-In (2), Qualifier wins (10).
- Homegrown U15–U19: league play, Pro Player Pathway, Flex.
- U13/U14: Quality of Play (QoP) standings.

The only possible link is **indirect**: AD Fest games may count toward regular-season standings (INFERRED from the AD Rules' "winter Events" wording).

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27: Homegrown Division expands from 32 to 48 teams in each of U15–U19, qualifying "through league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition, or MLS NEXT Flex presented by Allstate". U13/U14 qualify on QoP standings. Academy split is 20/2/10. Fest is not listed as a path. — [OFF-1](https://www.mlssoccer.com/mlsnext/news/mls-next-announces-key-dates-and-expanded-national-events-for-2026-27-season); [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- CONFIRMED, 2026–27: Flex runs Apr 29–May 4, 2027 in Atlanta, and group winners qualify automatically for the Cup. The Cup runs June 5–13, 2027 in Cincinnati. — [OFF-2](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/2026-27-mls-next-schedule-key-dates-to-know)
- AD Rules Sec. II.hh: the Regular Season is "inclusive of regional and winter MLS NEXT Academy Division Events, and counting towards points for regular season standings." — [OFF-4](https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1786468196/assets/documents/MLS_NEXT_AD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_jiohet.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Fest gives no direct Cup berth. For Academy teams, Fest results may affect league position, which feeds the 20 league spots and possibly the Play-In.
- For modelling, treat Fest as a showcase and do not count it as a qualification path. Flag the standings question as open.

### Gaps
- An explicit statement on whether Fest results count in AD or HD standings or QoP (U13/U14).
- The Homegrown Division rules PDF was not reviewed in this pass.
